My Calcium User Guide

🚀 Getting Started

Welcome to My Calcium!

My Calcium helps you track your daily calcium intake to support bone health and meet nutritional goals. All your data stays private on your device, and you can use the app offline.

First Steps

  1. Find Your Foods: Search the database, scan barcodes, or add custom foods with calcium content
  2. Log Your Intake: Add foods throughout the day with automatic serving size memory and favorites
  3. Track Progress: View daily totals, weekly trends, and long-term statistics to meet your goals
📊 Tracking Your Intake

Adding Foods

Tap the Add Food button to log calcium intake. You can search the USDA food database, scan product barcodes, or add custom foods.

Using the Food Database

Search for foods by name and select the appropriate serving size. The app remembers your preferred serving sizes for quick entry next time.

Barcode Scanning

Use the barcode scanner to quickly look up packaged foods. The app will fetch nutritional data from USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts.

Custom Foods

Create custom food entries for items not in the database. Set the calcium content per serving to track accurately.

Favorites

Mark frequently eaten foods as favorites for quick access. Swipe left on any food entry to reveal favorite and delete options.

📈 Statistics & Reports

Daily View

The main tracking page shows your daily calcium intake with a progress bar toward your goal. View all foods logged for the day.

Weekly & Monthly Trends

The Statistics page displays charts showing your calcium intake over time. Use these visualizations to identify patterns and maintain consistent nutrition.

Printable Reports

Generate printable reports of your calcium tracking history to share with healthcare providers or for personal records.

🗄️ Food Database

USDA Food Database

The app includes a curated database of common foods from the USDA FoodData Central, optimized for calcium content tracking.

Managing Custom Foods

View and edit all your custom food entries in the Database page. Edit or delete custom foods as needed.

Hiding Foods

Hide foods you don't eat from search results to streamline food selection. Hidden foods can be managed in the Database page.

⚙️ Settings & Preferences

Daily Goal

Set your personal daily calcium target (in mg) based on your nutritional needs and health provider recommendations. The default is 1000mg.

Appearance

Customize your app experience:

  • Theme: Choose Light, Dark, or Auto (follows system preference)
  • Color: Select your preferred accent color from Blue, Purple, Green, Orange, Red, or Teal

Data Management

  • Create Backup: Download a complete backup of your data including all entries, custom foods, and settings
  • Restore Data: Restore from a previously created backup file
  • Export to CSV: Export your food entries as a spreadsheet for analysis in Excel or Google Sheets

Sync (Optional)

Enable end-to-end encrypted sync to keep your data synchronized across multiple devices. Your data remains private - even the server cannot read it.

App Updates

Check for new versions in Settings > App > Check for Updates. When an update is available, tap Update App to install the latest version. Your data is always preserved during updates.

🔒 Privacy & Data

Local-First Design

All your data is stored directly on your device using IndexedDB. Nothing is sent to external servers unless you explicitly enable the optional sync feature.

Offline Functionality

My Calcium works completely offline after initial installation. Track your calcium intake anywhere, even without internet connectivity.

No Tracking or Analytics

This app contains no analytics, advertising, or user tracking. Your nutrition data is yours alone.

Encrypted Sync (Optional)

If you enable sync, all data is encrypted on your device before transmission. The server stores only encrypted data and cannot access your information.
